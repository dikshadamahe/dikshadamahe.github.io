# Monsoon Portfolio — Complete Asset Generation Sheet

Use this file to generate every visual and audio asset needed. Each entry has:
- **Filename** — save as this exact name in `portfolio/public/textures/` (images) or `portfolio/public/sounds/` (audio)
- **Prompt** — copy-paste into your image/sound generator
- **Specs** — dimensions, format, important notes
- **Reference** — attach `girl.png` character sheet where noted

**CRITICAL STYLE RULE:** Every illustrated asset must look like it belongs in the same world as the girl character. Match the warm, hand-painted, slightly textured storybook illustration style. Soft edges, warm undertones, not flat vector, not photorealistic.

## PART 9: SOUNDS

Download these from royalty-free sound libraries. Recommended sources: [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/), [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/).

---

### 9.1 — `rain-loop.mp3` (REQUIRED)

**What to search for:** "soft rain ambient loop" or "gentle monsoon rain" or "light rainfall ambience"

**Requirements:**
- Gentle, continuous rainfall — NOT heavy downpour, NOT thunderstorm
- Calming and atmospheric, like sitting under a tin roof during Indian monsoon
- 30-60 seconds long, must loop seamlessly (no abrupt start/end)
- No music, no voices, no traffic — just rain
- MP3 format, reasonable file size (< 2MB)

**Freesound search terms:** `rain gentle loop`, `soft rain ambient`, `monsoon rain light`

---

### 9.2 — `thunder.mp3` (REQUIRED)

**What to search for:** "distant thunder rumble" or "far away thunder"

**Requirements:**
- Distant, rolling thunder — NOT a close sharp crack
- Soft and atmospheric, not startling or scary
- 5-10 seconds long (single rumble that fades)
- Will be played at low volume at random intervals
- MP3 format, < 500KB

**Freesound search terms:** `distant thunder`, `far thunder rumble`, `soft thunder roll`

---

### 9.3 — `water-drops.mp3` (OPTIONAL)

**What to search for:** "water dripping" or "rain drops on surface"

**Requirements:**
- A few individual water drops hitting a surface
- 2-3 seconds, clean recording
- MP3, < 200KB

---

### 9.4 — `frog-croak.mp3` (OPTIONAL)

**What to search for:** "frog croak single" or "tree frog call"

**Requirements:**
- A single soft frog croak/ribbit
- 1-2 seconds
- Indian tree frog sound if possible, otherwise any small frog
- MP3, < 200KB

---

### 9.5 — `wet-footsteps.mp3` (OPTIONAL)

**What to search for:** "footsteps on wet road" or "walking in rain puddle"

**Requirements:**
- 3-5 light footsteps on a wet surface
- Should sound like a small person (child) walking, not heavy boots
- 2-3 seconds
- MP3, < 200KB
